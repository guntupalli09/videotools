## The CapCut caption problem (why people switch)

CapCut auto-captions look great inside the app — animated text, emoji hooks, brand fonts. That workflow breaks the moment you need:

1. **SRT or VTT file** for YouTube Studio, Vimeo, or a client deliverable  
2. **Separate subtitle track** viewers can toggle off  
3. **Translation to 70+ languages** as a proper subtitle file (not re-burned video)  
4. **Full transcript + chapters** for show notes or SEO  
5. **Re-use captions** across CapCut, Premiere, and YouTube without re-transcribing  

CapCut's captions are **project overlays**, not subtitle files. There is no CapCut SRT export on any plan — a fact that drives thousands of **capcut srt export** and **capcut alternative** searches every month.
