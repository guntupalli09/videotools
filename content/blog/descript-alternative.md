## What Descript does well (and when to keep it)

Descript wins when:

- You **edit video by editing transcript text**  
- You want **Overdub**, **Studio Sound**, screen recording in one app  
- You publish short-form from a single Descript project  

Descript loses when:

- You only need **deliverables** (SRT, DOCX, chapters) for an external editor  
- Your team already has an **NLE + asset pipeline**  
- You process **high volume** long-form and per-seat editor pricing hurts
