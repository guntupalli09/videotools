## Why people leave Otter.ai

Otter dominates **real-time meeting transcription** — calendar sync, OtterPilot, summaries in Slack. That is not what most **Otter alternative** searchers need.

Common triggers:

1. **Long video uploads** — Otter slows outside native meeting capture  
2. **YouTube / podcast workflows** — need chapters, show notes, SRT — Otter delivers conversation text  
3. **Minute caps** — Pro plans limit monthly transcription minutes; heavy file users hit walls  
4. **English-first** — multilingual file workflows push users to TurboScribe or VideoText  
5. **No guideline formatting** — freelance Rev/GoTranscript jobs need style-rule prep Otter does not offer
